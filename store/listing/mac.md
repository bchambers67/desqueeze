## Description

Photographs shot through an anamorphic lens come out squeezed. StillScope widens them back to
their true proportions on your Mac.

**Drop and see.** Drag a photo onto the window and move the factor control. The preview
follows live, and the common lens squeezes are one click away.

**Several at once.** Drop several photos and correct them all in one go, saving the results to a folder you choose.

**Keeps what the camera recorded.** Capture metadata is carried onto every file you save.

**Private.** StillScope runs in the App Sandbox, works only on files you choose, collects no
data and makes no network connections.
