## Description

Photographs shot through an anamorphic lens come out squeezed. StillScope stretches them back
to their true proportions, so the frame you saw is the frame you keep.

**Live correction.** Drag the factor slider and the preview follows at once. It snaps to the
common lens squeezes, and holding between two of them lets you fine-tune.

**Look closely.** Tap the photo to inspect it at 1:1, with pinch, pan and double-tap zoom.

**Find the squeezed ones for you.** Switch on Find squeezed photos and StillScope looks through
the photos you recently added or took, reading the lens the camera recorded, the shape of faces,
text and people, and the photos you have already corrected from the same session. It offers
to queue the ones that look squeezed. Everything happens on your device.

**One photo or a whole shoot.** Open one photo, or as many as you like. Each can follow one
factor, or take its own. Pick which photos to correct, and change the factor for all of the
selected ones at once.

**Keeps what the camera recorded.** Capture metadata travels with every saved photo. Add a thin
white matte, small or medium, if you want a border.

**Works where you do.** Use StillScope from the Share sheet in other apps, or edit directly
inside Photos. It is built for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo: on an open Duo held sideways the
controls sit at the edge and the factor slider stays on screen.

**Private.** StillScope does not collect data, show ads or track you. Photos are processed on
your device.

## Review notes (for App Review)

StillScope needs no account and no login. To try it:

1. Open the app and tap CHOOSE PHOTOS. Choose any photo. Drag the slider to change the factor.
   Tap the save button (bottom right) to write the corrected photo to the library.
2. Photos access: the app asks for permission to add photos when you first save. The optional
   "Find squeezed photos" feature (Settings) asks for read access to the library, to look at
   recent photos on the device. It is off until the user switches it on. Nothing leaves the device.
3. Any photo works for review. A photo shot through an anamorphic lens will look squeezed
   until corrected; any ordinary photo will simply be widened.
4. Share and Photos editing extensions: choose StillScope from the Share sheet in Photos, or
   from the Edit menu of a photo (Markup/extensions).
