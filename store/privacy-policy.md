# StillScope privacy policy

*Chambers & Light LLC. Effective [date].*

StillScope corrects photographs on your own device. It does not collect, store or share personal
information, and it contains no advertising or analytics.

**Photos.** StillScope processes the photos you choose, and, only if you switch on "Find squeezed
photos", the photos recently added to or taken in your library, entirely on your device. Photos
are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Photos stored in iCloud Photos may be downloaded to
your device by the system when you choose to correct them.

**Photo library access.** StillScope asks to add photos so that it can save corrected versions.
Read access is requested only if you switch on "Find squeezed photos".

**What stays on the device.** The app remembers its own settings, a list of photos it has
already looked at, and the factors you used, so that it can recognise photos from the same
shoot. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

**Children.** StillScope is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.
